# Artwork

The reroll badge adapts Kenney's `arrow_rotate.png` from
[Board Game Icons](https://kenney.nl/assets/board-game-icons), dedicated to the public domain
under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The original PNG and pinned mirror URL/SHA-256 are in `assets/`. The adaptation
centers the circular arrows in a 64×64 alpha mask, tinted gold on the dark-purple
and teal control. The mask is embedded in the DLL; no artwork downloads occur
in the game. The code's MIT license does not replace the artwork's CC0 dedication.
